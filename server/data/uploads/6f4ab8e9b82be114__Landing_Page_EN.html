Frequently asked questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

Generally one 40ft container per product; mixed loads are negotiable. See each product for details.

Which certifications can you provide?

HACCP, ISO 22000:2018, ASC, GlobalG.A.P, BRC Food, Mã vùng trồng & cơ sở đóng gói GACC, BAP (Best Aquaculture Practices), HALAL. Copies are shared on request.

Can you export to China through official channels?

Yes — we hold GACC-approved growing-area and packing-facility codes for official (chính ngạch) export.

Do you send samples before an order?

Yes. We ship representative samples so you can verify quality before committing.

What are your payment terms?

T/T or irrevocable L/C at sight; Incoterms FOB/CIF/CFR as agreed.
